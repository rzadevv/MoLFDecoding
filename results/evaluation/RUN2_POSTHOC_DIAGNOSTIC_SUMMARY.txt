MASK AUDIT
----------
The Run-2 gene reconstruction loss uses measured entries only:

  masked_error.sum() / mask.sum()

Therefore unmeasured Xenium genes are not treated as ground-truth
zeros in the gene-space reconstruction loss.

All seven Xenium test slides have substantially greater MoLF error

This indicates that the principal additional error arises in the
image-conditioned MoLF prediction / generated latent stage rather
than solely from the GeneVAE representation.


INTERPRETATION
--------------
Run-2 generalizes meaningful spatial structure but absolute
calibration is strongly technology-dependent.

Xenium has a substantially larger and denser gene-expression
target regime than Visium while MoLF is not explicitly conditioned
on spatial-transcriptomics technology.

The frozen GeneVAE is capable of reconstructing Xenium with high
relative fidelity. The largest degradation occurs when the latent
state must be predicted from the CONCH image representation.

Some Visium slides also show substantial MoLF-specific loss of
spatial structure, so the image-to-latent prediction limitation is
not exclusive to Xenium.


FUTURE HYPOTHESES
-----------------
For future model development, two distinct directions should be
considered:

1. Predict assay-specific measurements:
   explicitly condition the model on ST technology.

2. Predict technology-independent biology:
   construct a carefully harmonized cross-technology target space.

These are hypotheses for future work and were not used to alter
Run-2 after held-out test evaluation.
